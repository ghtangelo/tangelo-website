FAQ

Straight answers

What types of leads do you offer?

We provide leads for solar, roofing, bath and window contractors, nationwide.

How do I get started?

Fill out the form and tell us about your business. We'll set up a call to find the right package for you.

Can I choose where my leads come from?

Yes. You can customize your targeting by location and other criteria that fit your business. Keep in mind that narrower targeting can limit volume, so we'll help you find the right balance.

What information comes with each lead?

Each lead includes the homeowner's full name, phone number, email and address, plus project details specific to your industry to help your team prepare for the conversation.

How are leads delivered, and how fast?

Delivery happens after your payment is confirmed, through the method you choose: webhook, Excel or email. Leads usually start arriving within 36 hours, and we'll confirm timing for your order when we connect.

What if a lead isn't good?

Replacement and refund terms are spelled out in your package agreement, so you know exactly what's covered before you get started. We'll walk you through them on our call, and if you have a concern about a lead, tell us and we'll review it with you.

Where do your leads come from?

We source leads through a network of publishers and advertisers we work with.

Is there a long-term commitment?

No. There's no long-term commitment and no monthly retainer, and each order stands on its own. Every package comes with an insertion order and a written agreement that spells out the terms clearly and protects you, us and the consumer. We'll go over both with you on our call before you get started.
